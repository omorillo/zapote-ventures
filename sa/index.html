Governance by design

Governance by architecture, not by prompt

The LLM is only one component. Enterprise-grade agentic AI requires independent controls for identity, authorization, policy enforcement, tool access, monitoring and human oversight.

We separate intelligence from control. The model may propose an action, but permissions, business policies and human approval requirements determine whether it is actually executed.
